I'm an Assistant Professor of Global Change in the Department of Integrative Biology at the [University of South Florida St. Petersburg](https://www.usf.edu/usf-st-petersburg/), where I lead the **[Maitner Lab](https://maitnerlab.github.io/)**.

My research focuses on the origin, maintenance, and conservation of biodiversity. I build bigger and better datasets and new computational tools to tackle long-standing ecological questions and pressing conservation problems. I work across scales, from a few species in a small plot to whole phyla across the globe, and across taxa, mostly plants and terrestrial vertebrates.

I believe in an open, inclusive, and collaborative approach to science. I help develop the [BIEN](https://bien.nceas.ucsb.edu/bien/) plant database and several [R packages](/software/) for working with biodiversity data, and I've co-led international field courses on plant functional traits.

**For the lab's research, people, and openings for prospective students, see the [Maitner Lab website](https://maitnerlab.github.io/).**
