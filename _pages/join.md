The Maitner Lab at the [University of South Florida, St. Petersburg](https://www.usf.edu/usf-st-petersburg/) is looking for curious, motivated people who want to understand and protect biodiversity. Our work combines Big Data, computational tools, and field research, from small plots to the whole globe. See our [Research](/research/) page for current projects.

We value an inclusive, collaborative lab. People from groups underrepresented in science, and those with non-traditional paths into research, are strongly encouraged to get in touch.
