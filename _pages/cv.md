## Positions

- **2024–present:** Assistant Professor of Global Change, Department of Integrative Biology, University of South Florida St. Petersburg
- **2024:** Postdoctoral Scholar, University of Connecticut. Species distribution models for poorly sampled species, and the velocity and direction of climate-driven range shifts.
- **2022–2024:** Terrestrial sampling designer, [BioSCape](https://www.bioscape.io/), NASA's first biodiversity-focused field campaign. Led the selection of its terrestrial sites in South Africa's Cape Floristic Region.
- **2021–2024:** Postdoctoral Scholar, University at Buffalo. Remote sensing and Bayesian workflows to detect vegetation change (fire, drought, invasions, land clearing) for land managers in the Cape Floristic Region.
- **2020–2021:** Postdoctoral Scholar, University of Connecticut. New global ecoregions based on plant distribution models, their conservation status, and projected changes.
