## University of South Florida

**Biometry** (PCB 6455 / BSC 4933)  
Statistical ecology in R, following Ben Bolker's *Ecological Models and Data in R*. Course materials are openly available on [GitHub](https://github.com/bmaitner/biometry_course) and archived on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17923198).

**Statistical Ecology** (PCB 6455 / BSC 4933)  
A lighter, 3-credit version of Biometry (which is 4 credits), introducing exploratory data analysis and statistical modeling in ecology with R. Course materials are on [GitHub](https://github.com/bmaitner/Statistical_ecology_course).

## Plant Functional Traits Courses

I co-led international [Plant Functional Traits Course](https://plantfunctionaltraitscourses.w.uib.no/) expeditions in China, Svalbard, Peru, Norway, and South Africa. Each course brought together 25 to 50 participants from more than 10 countries to learn trait-based ecology through hands-on field work, data management, and analysis.
