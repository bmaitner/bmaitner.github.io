My course materials are openly licensed so others can reuse them.

## University of South Florida

**Biometry** (PCB 6455 / BSC 4933), 2026  
Statistical ecology in R, following Ben Bolker's *Ecological Models and Data in R*. Materials on [GitHub](https://github.com/bmaitner/biometry_course).

**Statistical Ecology** (PCB 6455 / BSC 4933), 2025  
A lighter, 3-credit version of Biometry (which is 4 credits), introducing exploratory data analysis and statistical modeling in ecology with R. Materials on [GitHub](https://github.com/bmaitner/Statistical_ecology_course) and archived on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17923198).

**Community Ecology**, 2025 and 2026  
Materials on [GitHub](https://github.com/bmaitner/Community_ecology_course) and archived on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15359465).

## Plant Functional Traits Courses

I co-led international [Plant Functional Traits Course](https://plantfunctionaltraitscourses.w.uib.no/) expeditions in China (2015, 2016), Peru (2018, 2020), Norway (2018, 2022), and South Africa (2023). Each course brought together 25 to 50 participants from more than 10 countries to learn trait-based ecology through hands-on field work, data management, and analysis.
